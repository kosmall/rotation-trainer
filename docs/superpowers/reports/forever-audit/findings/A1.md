# A1: fight engine (audit findings)

Auditor: A1. Files: `src/forever/fight.ts`, `runner.ts`, `ltpolicy.ts`, `rotations.ts`, `rotation-spec.ts`, `rotation-genes.ts`,
and the params they read (`src/forever/data/params.json`). Helpers read for context only: `policy.ts`, `stats.ts`, `spells.ts`, `effects.ts`, `data/spells.json`, `data/talents.json`.
Source of truth: `docs/forever-data-ledger.md` (sections 1, 3, 6, 7) and the `source` fields of `talents.json`/`spells.json`
(note: `src/data/classes/warlock/talentDescriptions.json` is a **TBC** tooltip file, not Forever; it was not used as a source).
No repository file was changed except this one.

Reproduction scripts and the patched copies of `fight.ts`/`runner.ts` used for "what if" numbers (all outside the repo), directory
`/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-rotation-trainer/05276e97-9c03-5b59-93ad-fdb2660cdf40/scratchpad/`:
`r1_est.ts`, `r1b.ts`, `r1c.ts`, `r2_pushback.ts`, `r3_petmana.ts`, `r3b.ts`, `r3c.ts`, `r4.ts`, `r5_alt.ts`, `r6_pandemic.ts`,
`r7_dotlogic.py`, `r8_cancel.ts`, `r9_parity.ts`, `r10_rest.ts`, `r11_chfix.ts`, `r12_sh.ts`, `r13_sac.ts`, `r14_misc.ts`, `r15_sh.ts`, `r16_lt.ts`,
`p/*.ts` (patched copies). Run with `npx tsx <file>` from the repo root. Seeds are fixed; numbers below are the printed output.

Summary: the core event loop (event order, GCD, cast and channel timing, Bane and Improved Corruption reductions, Wrack's DoT acceleration,
Life Tap thresholds, break rules, mana/HP/pet carry between pulls) matches the ledger. Findings: (a) three of nine "second filler" conditions
can never fire in the fight (`mana_lt_30`, `mana_lt_50`, `late`), (b) the filler damage estimate ignores channel length, (c) Demonic Sacrifice
regeneration is not applied between pulls, (d) a few smaller order/accounting gaps, (e) a set of unsourced model constants listed for the owner.

## Checked and found correct

Event order and loop (fight.ts:751-848)
- Order inside one timestamp: cast completion, channel ticks, DoT ticks, pet actions, mob swings; decisions happen after all events of that instant. DoT whose last tick is due at the expiry still ticks (`DOT_EPS_MS`), then is dropped.
- GCD 1.5 s from `gcd_ms` (matches ledger), started at cast start, casts shorter than the GCD wait for it, casts longer than it free the player at the cast end; instant spells and Life Tap are on the GCD; waste accounting `gcdWasteMs` is right. A target dying in a cast gives back the unused busy time but keeps the GCD.
- Cast time: Bane -0.1 s per rank only for `banable` spells (Shadow Bolt, Immolate; not Searing Pain, Corruption), Shadow Bolt rank 1 at 5/5 = 1.2 s, Immolate 1.5 s; Improved Corruption -0.4 s per rank and instant at 5/5 (ledger: 2 s cast). Shadow Trance: next Shadow Bolt instant, mana still paid (ledger section 2).
- Channels: Wrack 6 s / 1 s ticks / 200 mana, Drain Life 5 s / 1 s, Drain Soul 15 s / 3 s; tick damage, SP coefficients per tick (0.286 x 0.7 = 0.2002 for Wrack, 0.1, 0.1) as in the ledger; Wrack's damage formula (A1-013); Improved Drains +20% at 3/3 on Drain Life/Soul but not Wrack; Soul Siphon +4% per rank per effect, counts only Corruption/Siphon Life/Agony, max 36%, on Drain Life/Soul/Wrack; Soul Harvest granted when Drain Soul is the channel when the mob dies.

- Nightfall: 2% per rank per tick of Corruption, Drain Soul, Drain Life and Wrack (not Siphon Life, not Agony), Shadow Trance consumed by the next Shadow Bolt; proc counted per tick, so Wrack's doubled Corruption ticks proc twice as often.
