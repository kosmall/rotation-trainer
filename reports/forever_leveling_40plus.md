# WoW Forever: warlock leveling builds 40-59 (simulation report)

Generated by `npm run forever:report` (seed 1; 100 sessions of 30 pulls per cell; the sensitivity analysis uses 40 sessions per cell). Same-level normal mobs, 1 mob per pull unless stated, gear profile **average** unless stated.

**Life Tap and break policy (the same for every build):** Life Tap when mana is below 60% and HP above 80%, or mana below 20% and HP above 30%; between mobs also when HP is 100% and mana below 90%. The only break is for food and water when HP falls below 30%; it lasts until HP is full and the warlock drinks only while eating. Life Tap is instant, so casting it on the walk to the next mob costs no time while the walk lasts. Out-of-combat HP regeneration is assumed to be zero (not found).

> **Read this first.** Spell ranks (mana, cast, ticks, spell power modifiers), talent effects and pet spells come from Wowhead Forever spell pages, warcraft.wiki.gg, guildorder, allthings, timesaver and Blizzard hotfix notes (25 Nov 2026; see `docs/forever-data-ledger.md`). **Not found anywhere, so still estimates flagged `verify: true`:** mob health and damage by level, pet melee damage and health, Spirit regeneration, drink and food rates, Life Tap's exact formula, gear stats, Wrack's level scaling and the Bane of Agony ramp. The ranking is only as good as these; the sensitivity section shows which can change the winner. The TODO list is in the ledger.

## 8. What is not modelled

- Pet resurrection costs (shards), Soul Shards in general, Health Funnel, Fear/crowd control and kiting, movement, line of sight and travel time of spells.
- Gold cost and time of the respec, vendor trips, bags and repairs; elites, rares, quest mobs with special mechanics.
- Threat is simplified to two numbers per mob (player and pet) with the 110%/130% overtake rule; Demonic Brand, Searing Pain and Voidwalker Torment feed it.
- Pet HP and mana are refilled before every pull; a dead pet costs `pet_resummon_sec`.
- Mob HP is fixed per level (no variance), same level as the player, melee only, one swing every 2 s.
- Damage multipliers combine multiplicatively (the game may add some of them).
- Haste is off by default (`haste_pct` 0) and does not shorten the GCD unless `haste_shortens_gcd` is on.
