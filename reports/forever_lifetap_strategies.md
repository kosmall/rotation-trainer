# Life Tap strategies for WoW Forever leveling (40-59)

Generated by `npm run forever:lifetap`. Gear: average, 1 mob(s) per pull (sections 3-4; section 5 varies it), levels 40, 45, 50, 55, 59; 60 sessions of 30 pulls per cell, seed 1. Score = mean kills per hour over builds x levels. Mobs, player base stats, regeneration and pets use WoW Classic data (cmangos) with Forever drink and food tooltips; gear stats are placeholders; Forever raised creature health by an unknown factor, tested in section 5. Data caveats are in `docs/forever-data-ledger.md`.

## 1. The strategies

| Strategy | Rule |
|---|---|
| USER | The user's rule: in combat mana<60% & HP>80%, or mana<20% & HP>30%; between mobs also HP=100% & mana<90%; break only below 30% HP, drinking only while eating. |
| OLD_THRESHOLD | First version of this simulator: Life Tap in combat only at mana<35% & HP>60%, no Life Tap between mobs; stop and drink whenever mana<40% or HP<50%, then drink and eat up to 90%. |
| NO_LIFE_TAP | Never Life Tap; stop and drink at mana<25% or HP<30%, drink to full. |
| COMBAT_ONLY | User's rule without the between-mobs Life Tap. |
| BETWEEN_ONLY | No Life Tap in combat; between mobs tap whenever HP>=60% and mana<100%; break below 30% HP. |
| AGGRESSIVE_BETWEEN | User's rule, but between mobs tap down to 60% HP whenever mana<100%. |
| CAUTIOUS | User's rule with stricter HP (combat tap needs HP>90%, break below 40% HP). |
